Welcome to Kwa-Mzilikazi

Located at 43 Eloff Street in the Gandhi Square precinct, Kwa-Mzilikazi has become a go-to spot for CBD workers, students and residents looking for an affordable, home-style meal. Every dish is prepared fresh, the same way it would be at home.

What We Offer

  • Home-cooked stews, tripe and traditional sides
  • Dine-in seating and takeaway packaging
  • Fresh meat bones sold by the kilogram
  • Cold refreshments and soft drinks
