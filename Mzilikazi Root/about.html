About Kwa-Mzilikazi

Our Story

Kwa-Mzilikazi is a home-style eatery based at 43 Eloff Street, in the Gandhi Square precinct of Johannesburg's inner city. What started as a small kitchen serving CBD workers has grown into a well-known local stop for anyone craving an honest, home-cooked plate of food. Alongside the kitchen, Kwa-Mzilikazi sells fresh meat bones and cold refreshments to the surrounding community.

Our Mission

To serve affordable, authentic home-cooked meals that bring comfort and community to Johannesburg's inner city.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted home-style eatery in the Johannesburg CBD, known for consistency, value and warm hospitality.

The People Behind the Food

Kwa-Mzilikazi is run by a small, dedicated kitchen team who prepare every dish fresh daily, using recipes passed down through generations.
